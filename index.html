// HELLO!

I am Born and raised in the Province of La Union, I am a Fresh IT Graduate with specialization in Multimedia Arts From Don Mariano Marcos Memorial State University Mid-La Union Camps, Catbangen, SFCLU. As a creative professional and curious thinker, I am driven by a passion for understanding how things work and a desire to brainstorm, innovative solutions that improve lives. I specialize in transforming abstract ideas into high-impact, aesthetically pleasing visual stories. My creative process is rooted in an end-to-end production approach: from capturing raw imagery and video in the field to executing strategic post-production editing and graphic design. I believe that great Multimedia is a blend of technical discipline and empathetic design. Whether I am behind the camera, editing promotional video content, or designing advocacy posters, my goal is always the same. to create visuals that do not just lookk good, but also communicate with clarity, purpose, and impact.
